At the lowest chamber of the machine, beneath the calendars and progress bars, there is an instruction engraved in the dark:

`NOP`

It receives the current.
It occupies the cycle.
It alters nothing.

The counter rises anyway.

One billion false heartbeats each second pass through the terminal, counted by a clock that does not know your name and cannot explain why you are waiting.

The screen empties.
The cursor disappears.
The apparatus continues its ancient liturgy of measurement.

For one appointed interval, you offer it no task.

The ledger receives your silence and records nothing.
